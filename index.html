Glowrova — AI Beauty & Grooming Platform for Tier-2 India

Glowrova is an AI-driven beauty & grooming platform designed specifically for Tier-2 Bharat. While metros are saturated with generic booking marketplaces, neighbourhood salons in Tier-2 cities suffer from long walk-in queues, paper registers, and zero digital scheduling tools. Glowrova connects customers with verified neighbourhood salons through smart chair reservations, WhatsApp booking flows, and AI face/hair consultations.

Customer Platform (glowrova.in) Partner Platform (glowrova.partner.in)
