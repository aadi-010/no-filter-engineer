02 BUILD NOTES: WHY NO FILTER

Build it in the open, keep what's real, ship every week

Most tech narratives are told in retrospect: sanitized, cinematic, and scrubbed of doubt. Starting from Gaya, Bihar, with no industry pedigree, I chose to document the raw build in public. The broken migrations, the awkward user calls, the architecture pivots—everything stays on tape. Real leverage comes from radical honesty.

NOTE 01 · ORIGIN

From Gaya to Hyderabad

Tracing the trajectory from a small ancient city in Bihar to Hyderabad’s tech core. Moving hundreds of miles with a laptop and a single conviction: building scalable digital rails for Bharat's unorganized local markets.

NOTE 02 · DEMOGRAPHY

Tier-2 first

While metros suffer from hyper-saturated salon marketplaces, Tier-2 cities hold high-frequency salon demand with zero structured queueing infrastructure.

NOTE 03 · PRODUCT

AI that books the chair

Glowrova gives every neighbourhood salon an autonomous booking agent that turns casual WhatsApp voice notes into confirmed chair reservations—eliminating walk-in queue fatigue.
